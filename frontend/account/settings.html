Reset All Progress

Resetting your progress delete all question history and quiz scores.

Resetting all progress will:
  • Reset all course progress, question history, and quiz scores to zero.

Deactivate Account

Deactivating your account can be temporary.

Deactivating your account will:
  • Disable your profile from public view.
  • Allow you to restore your account at any time by logging back in.

Delete Account

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Deleting your account will:
  • Permanently delete your profile and erase account information.
  • Remove all progress and earned points, which cannot be recovered.
